Methods

This page was generated by scripts/build_reports.py on 7 October 2026 from the data snapshot described below. No number is entered by hand.

  • Studies. Records in data/long-covid.json (source: PubMed (NCBI); feed last updated 2026-07-07) whose pub_date parses to a date in 2026. Year-only or year-month dates are mapped to the first day of the period. 0 record(s) had no parseable date and were excluded. Monthly counts bin by publication month; journal counts use the record's journal string verbatim (no normalisation of abbreviations).
  • Trials. Records in data/clinical_trials/clinical_trials_current.json (snapshot 2026-06-26). A trial matches when its mapped_conditions contains 'Long COVID / PASC'. Status, phase and sponsor type are the registry's own fields, counted once per trial. "Started in 2026" uses start_date; "updated in 2026" uses last_updated. Agents are the tracker's normalised intervention names; a trial with several agents counts once for each. Median enrollment uses the registry enrollment field (actual or estimated) where numeric.
  • Agents catalogue. Entries in data/therapeutic_agents.json (updated 2026-07-09) whose "Primary Conditions" includes 'Long COVID', grouped by the catalogue's "Evidence Level" string.
  • Biomarkers. Entries in data/biomarkers/long-covid.json, grouped by category and direction (up, down, mixed). Distinct DOIs count unique reference.doi values.
  • Cohorts. Entries in data/pais-cohorts-index.json (version 2.0.0-seed) whose pathogen_id is in ['sars-cov-2'].

Limitations

  • The literature feed is a bounded, rolling PubMed query result. It captures recent indexing activity, not the full year, and PubMed indexing dates lag print publication. Counts here will not match a systematic search.
  • Study counts measure volume, not quality. No screening for study design, sample size, peer-review status or relevance beyond the search query was applied.
  • Trial matching depends on the tracker's condition mapping and keyword patterns; trials registered under umbrella terms (for example "post-viral fatigue") may be missed, and registry status fields are frequently stale.
  • Biomarker direction reflects what each cited source reported, often from a single cohort; "elevated" in one study does not imply replication.
  • The cohort database and agents catalogue are curated and incomplete, and the evidence-level grading is automatic and coarse.
  • Different sections rest on different snapshot dates (feed 2026-07-07, trials 2026-06-26, biomarkers 2026-06-29, agents 2026-07-09).
  • This is a year-to-date report; it is regenerated daily and will change until 31 December 2026.
