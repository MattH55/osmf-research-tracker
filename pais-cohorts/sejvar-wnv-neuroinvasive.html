Sejvar West Nile Neuroinvasive Disease Cohort

source record (JSON) · schema v2.0.0 · CC BY 4.0

Design notes & limitations. Small prospective inception cohort of confirmed neuroinvasive disease with structured serial assessment to 90 days. Short follow-up and no control group; captures acute-to-subacute neurological recovery.
