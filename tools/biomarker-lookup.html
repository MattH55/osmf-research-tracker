Biomarker lookup

Flag your lab results as high, low or normal and see which post-viral and chronic illness conditions report those markers moving the same way in peer-reviewed research.

This is not a diagnostic tool. It cross-references the direction of change (higher / lower than controls) reported in research cohorts. It does not know your reference ranges, does not interpret any number you type, and cannot tell you whether you have a condition. Many common markers are altered in dozens of unrelated illnesses. Use the output only as a conversation starter with a qualified clinician.

      Frequently asked questions

      What does this tool do?

      You add lab markers to a panel and mark each as high, low or normal. The tool ranks the 25 conditions in the OSMF biomarker atlases by how many of your flagged markers are reported moving in the same direction in peer-reviewed research, and links to the atlas page and to the evidence page for each matched marker.

      What does it not do?

      It does not diagnose anything, does not interpret the numbers you type, and does not apply reference ranges. The data records only direction of change in research cohorts, not thresholds, sensitivity or specificity. Overlap with published findings is all a score means. CRP, ferritin and IL-6 are altered in dozens of unrelated conditions.

      Where does the data come from?

      From the OSMF biomarker atlases: structured JSON files (data/biomarkers/<condition>.json) built from systematic reviews and meta-analyses, with a DOI citation, comparison group and LOINC code for each marker where available. This page fetches those same files directly, so it always shows what the atlases show.

      Is anything I enter stored or sent anywhere?

      No. Everything runs in your browser. Your panel is encoded only in the page address after the #, so a link you copy contains your flags and any values you typed. Share it only with people you want to see it. We do not receive it.

      How do I cite this?

      Open Source Medicine Foundation. Biomarker lookup: match your lab results to post-viral and chronic illness research. Research Tracker. https://research.opensourcemed.info/tools/biomarker-lookup.html (accessed date). Cite individual studies through the DOI links on each marker.
