Research on post-viral and post-infectious syndromes beyond COVID-19, Lyme disease, and Gulf War Illness. Includes post-viral fatigue syndromes triggered by Epstein-Barr virus (EBV), cytomegalovirus (CMV), enteroviruses, influenza, and other pathogens. Also covers post-infectious autonomic dysfunction, viral encephalitis sequelae, and conditions that mechanistically overlap with ME/CFS and Long COVID.
