Where the gaps are

Outcomes not found as a primary outcome in a group's trials

  • Among the 8 trials of cognitive training and education found, none used clinical events, survival and healthcare use as a primary outcome (7 trials of other approaches did).
  • Among the 10 trials of supplements and nutraceuticals found, none used symptom severity and disease activity as a primary outcome (3 trials of other approaches did).

Questions studied but with no results posted

  • Exercise and physical activity × inflammation and other biomarkers: 3 trials registered, none with results posted (1 still recruiting)
  • Cognitive training and education × feasibility, adherence and safety: 3 trials registered, none with results posted (1 still recruiting)
  • Supplements and nutraceuticals × clinical events, survival and healthcare use: 3 trials registered, none with results posted

How outcomes are grouped

Each trial's primary outcome measures are sorted into domains by keyword rules. 17 trials for this condition have primary outcomes the rules could not classify; they appear in the last column rather than being dropped. Checked against 90 hand-labelled outcomes; on a held-out sample of 30, the rules agreed with the hand label for 20 before adjustment, and every outcome they labelled was labelled correctly (misses were left unclassified).

Trial selection and exclusions follow the non-drug evidence map method. Source: ClinicalTrials.gov, searched 2026-11-16.
