Asymmetric Dimethylarginine (ADMA) across conditions

Asymmetric Dimethylarginine (ADMA) appears in 2 entries of the OSMF biomarker atlas, covering Hypertension, Long COVID. Across those entries it is elevated in 1, mixed in 1. Because the same marker shifts in several unrelated conditions, it is best understood as a signal of shared biology such as inflammation, vascular or metabolic stress rather than as a condition-specific test. Frequently asked questions

In which conditions is Asymmetric Dimethylarginine (ADMA) altered?

In the OSMF atlas Asymmetric Dimethylarginine (ADMA) is reported in Hypertension, Long COVID. It is elevated in 1, mixed in 1 across 2 entries.

Does Asymmetric Dimethylarginine (ADMA) point to one specific diagnosis?

No. Because Asymmetric Dimethylarginine (ADMA) changes in several conditions (Hypertension, Long COVID), an abnormal result cannot identify which condition, if any, is present. It must be interpreted with symptoms, history and other tests.

What is the LOINC code for Asymmetric Dimethylarginine (ADMA)?

Entries in this atlas use LOINC 10988-1. LOINC codes identify the laboratory observation so results can be compared across systems.

Is one abnormal Asymmetric Dimethylarginine (ADMA) result diagnostic?

No single biomarker result is diagnostic of any of these conditions. Findings reflect group averages from published cohorts with substantial overlap between patients and controls.
