B. burgdorferi EIA (screening) in Lyme Disease

B. burgdorferi EIA (screening), also described as Enzyme immunoassay — tier 1, belongs to the serologic group of Lyme Disease (Lyme borreliosis, Borrelia burgdorferi infection) markers and is reported to be raised relative to Uninfected. Clinically, it has been associated with active or past Lyme infection. The finding is drawn from CDC 2024; it describes group-level differences, not a threshold that classifies an individual.

Frequently asked questions

Is B. burgdorferi EIA (screening) high or low in Lyme Disease?

Elevated. Studies summarised in the OSMF atlas report higher B. burgdorferi EIA (screening) in Lyme Disease than in Uninfected. Source: CDC 2024.

What symptoms is B. burgdorferi EIA (screening) associated with in Lyme Disease?

The cited literature associates this marker with Active or past Lyme infection. These are population-level associations and do not mean the marker causes the symptoms.

Which test measures B. burgdorferi EIA (screening)?

It is measured as a blood test (serum / plasma / whole blood). Commercial laboratories such as LabCorp, Quest Diagnostics list an orderable assay.

Is one abnormal B. burgdorferi EIA (screening) result diagnostic of Lyme Disease?

No. No single biomarker is diagnostic of Lyme Disease. B. burgdorferi EIA (screening) findings come from group comparisons, overlap considerably between patients and controls, and vary with study design, timing and comparison group. A result should be interpreted by a clinician alongside symptoms, history and other tests.
