International archives of allergy and immunology · 22 November 2026 · Okumus Guclu, Aysu, Seren, Orbay Tutku, Ozaydn Unal, Sultan, Karatas, Hakan Ömer et al. PubMed 43066631

Chronic spontaneous urticaria (CSU) is often refractory, and some patients do not achieve control with second-generation H1-antihistamines (sgAHs). Basophil Activation Testing: From Diagnostic Adjunct to Precision Allergy Management.

Allergy · 22 November 2026 · Catamerò, Francesco, Han, Jooeun, Karabacak, Deniz Eyice, Hoffmann, Hans Jürgen et al. PubMed 43068402

Basophils are rare circulating granulocytes that trigger immediate hypersensitivity reactions through IgE-mediated degranulation and also contribute to innate immunity. The basophil activation test (BAT) is a flow cytometry-based functional assay that quantifies basophil reactivity and sensitivity following allergen stimulation, offering a safe, blood-based alternative to in vivo provocation testing.
