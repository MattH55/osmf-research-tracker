Long COVID (PASC) 12 new

Full feed: Long COVID research tracker

Treatment strategies in pancreatic adenosquamous carcinoma: a systematic review and pooled analysis of individual cases.

Journal of gastrointestinal oncology · 8 November 2026 · Gao, Xiang Yu, Ganatra, Nency, Abdelhakeem, Ahmed, Tran, Nugyen et al. PubMed 42996569

Pancreatic adenosquamous carcinoma (PASC) is a rare 1-4% of all pancreatic cancers and an aggressive pancreatic malignancy characterized by mixed adenocarcinoma and squamous histologies. Even after surgical intervention, patients with PASC have a lower median overall survival (mOS) than those with the more prevalent pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC).

Persistently activated virus-specific CD8 T cells indicate antigen-driven immune aberrations in long COVID.

bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology · 8 November 2026 · Painter, Mark M, Reeg, David B, Douek, Naomi R, Gray-Gaillard, Sophie L et al. PubMed 42995851

Long COVID (LC) affects millions of people worldwide, yet its driving mechanisms remain incompletely defined and no treatment has been approved. Persistence of SARS-CoV-2 and reactivation of herpesviruses have been implicated in LC, but direct detection of these viral antigens in blood and tissues has been challenging.

Long COVID: A Modified Delphi Consensus on Definition, Burden, Diagnosis and Management in Southeast Asia.

Pulmonary therapy · 8 November 2026 · Burhan, Erlina, Chew, Huck Chin, Isbaniah, Fathiyah, Jogulu, Sathya Rao et al. PubMed 42998216

Long COVID is a heterogeneous post-infectious condition following acute severe acute respiratory syndrome-coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2) infection. Ongoing uncertainty around its definition, burden, diagnosis and management complicates clinical recognition and care in Southeast Asia.

Quality of life research : an international journal of quality of life aspects of treatment, care and rehabilitation · 8 November 2026 · Pimminger, Theresa, Ahrens-Schwabe, Annika, Fragner, Tobias, Brandl, Tobias et al. PubMed 42998183

Post-exertional malaise (PEM) is a multidimensional exertion-response pattern involving diverse triggers, variable onset, and prolonged recovery. This study developed and content-validated the Vienna Post-Exertional Malaise (VPEM) Questionnaire, a patient-reported outcome measure assessing functional decline, recovery, crash-associated symptoms, and pacing adaptations in ME/CFS.

Journal of proteome research · 6 November 2026 · Lu, Qiuhan, Zhang, Yi, Yi, Shiyu, Wu, Yuwen et al. PubMed 42992899

Sex differences in COVID-19 and its sequelae remain poorly understood, which hinders targeted therapies. We performed plasma proteomic analyses of 69 hospitalized patients with COVID-19 and 30 healthy controls from a prospective Chinese cohort.

Incidence and risk factors of acute respiratory illness associated with influenza, COVID-19, and respiratory syncytial virus within a birth cohort study.

Journal of virology · 6 November 2026 · Bird, Bryden, Thorn, Louise, Aminisani, Nayyereh, Wood, Tim et al. PubMed 42992825

Influenza virus, severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2), and respiratory syncytial virus (RSV) are leading causes of acute respiratory infections (ARIs). New Zealand's coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) public health measures disrupted the circulation of these viruses in 2020-2021, creating a rare opportunity to explore the impact of potential immunity gaps on age-specific infection risk.

Chronic Lyme / Post-Treatment Lyme Disease Syndrome 1 new

Full feed: Lyme / PTLDS research tracker

Cardiovascular Manifestations of Lyme Disease.

Cardiology in review · 8 November 2026 · Manolis, Antonis A, Manolis, Theodora A, Vouliotis, Apostolos, Manolis, Antonis S PubMed 42995416

Lyme disease is a tick-borne zoonosis caused by Borrelia burgdorferi that can involve multiple organ systems, including the cardiovascular (CV) system, noted in ~1-10% of untreated patients. This can produce atrioventricular (AV) or bundle branch block, atrial fibrillation, and myopericarditis in the acute phase, and dilated cardiomyopathy in the chronic phase.
