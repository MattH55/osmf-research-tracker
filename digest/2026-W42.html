SARS-CoV-2 seeds IAPP aggregation and β-cell failure.

Signal transduction and targeted therapy · 9 October 2026 · Song, Zhiqi, Xu, Yanfeng, Zhao, Wenjie, Zhang, Ling et al. PubMed 42850229

The global diabetes incidence has surged post-COVID-19, yet how SARS-CoV-2 drives long-term beta cell (β-cell) failure remains incompletely elucidated. We identified a mechanism whereby SARS-CoV-2 structural proteins directly hijack islet amyloid polypeptide (IAPP) to induce pathogenic amyloid deposition, disrupting insulin granule maturation, trafficking, and secretion in an age-susceptible islet microenvironment.
