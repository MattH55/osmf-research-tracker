Hospital Readmissions in Patients with Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome: A Retrospective Study in Northeastern Brazil.

Diseases (Basel, Switzerland) · 24 September 2026 · Wanderley, Raphael Omena, Dos Santos, Carmina Silva, Agra, Karine Ferreira, Gonçalves et al. PubMed 42783201

Severe acute respiratory syndrome (SARS) remains an important cause of hospitalization and mortality, particularly among vulnerable populations. Understanding the factors associated with previous hospitalization may support more effective prevention and vaccination strategies.
