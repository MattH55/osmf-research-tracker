Remission & chronicity

Spontaneous remission
CIN2/3 (precancerous): spontaneous regression in 40-60% of CIN2 without treatment; CIN3: regression in ~30%; invasive cervical cancer: no spontaneous regression
Best-intervention remission
Early-stage (IA-IIA): radical hysterectomy or chemoradiation — 80-90% 5-year survival; locally advanced (IIB-IVA): cisplatin-based chemoradiation — 60-70% 5-year survival; metastatic with pembrolizumab + chemotherapy ± bevacizumab: 24.4 vs 16.3 months OS (KEYNOTE-826); ENGOT-cx11/GOG-3047: cemiplimab + chemo superior

Gap size
Very large from a prevention standpoint — HPV vaccination and screening can prevent most cervical cancers but coverage is dramatically insufficient
Primary barrier
access (prevention)
Barrier detail: HPV vaccination prevents ~90% of cervical cancers caused by HPV 16/18 (and gardasil 9 covers 9 types); cervical cancer screening (Pap + HPV co-testing) prevents invasive cancer; yet globally only 10% of women in LMICs have received the HPV vaccine; in the US, HPV vaccination coverage remains at ~60% of adolescents

Last SoC change: 2022 — cemiplimab approved as first-line for PD-L1+ advanced/metastatic CC; 2021 pembrolizumab as first-line (KEYNOTE-826); tisotumab vedotin (ADC) 2021; cisplatin chemoradiation as standard since 1999
