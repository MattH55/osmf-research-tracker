Remission & chronicity

Spontaneous remission
~90% of acute LBP episodes resolve within 6 weeks regardless of treatment; chronic LBP (>3 months) shows only 30-40% recovery at 1 year and high recurrence; opioid prescribing correlates inversely with recovery
Best-intervention remission
Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) + graded exercise: 50-70% sustained improvement at 1 year in chronic LBP; Pain Reprocessing Therapy (PRT) — 66% pain-free or nearly pain-free at 1 year (JAMA Psychiatry 2021, vs 20% placebo); early active rehabilitation (vs bed rest) dramatically reduces chronification

Primary barrier
practice
Barrier detail: Opioid prescribing for chronic LBP is associated with worse long-term outcomes yet remains extremely common; spinal fusion surgery rates 40x higher in US than UK despite no superiority over conservative care in most indications; active rehabilitation demonstrably superior to passive treatment but requires patient effort and provider time

Last SoC change: 2017 — ACP guidelines explicitly recommended against opioids as first-line for chronic LBP; 2016 CDC opioid prescribing guidelines; imaging-guided approach deprioritized; exercise and CBT as first-line since 2017 — but implementation is poor
