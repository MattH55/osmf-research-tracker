Remission & chronicity

Spontaneous remission
Essentially zero; MGUS (precursor) progresses to myeloma in 1%/year; median survival without treatment <1 year once symptomatic
Best-intervention remission
Daratumumab + VRd (daratumumab + bortezomib + lenalidomide + dexamethasone) induction + autologous SCT: 66% MRD-negative at 24 months (PERSEUS); CAR-T (idecabtagene vicleucel, ciltacabtagene autoleucel): 71-75% overall response, 28-33% CR in heavily pretreated patients; 5-year survival now ~55% (vs ~20% in 2000)
Drug-free remission
—

Chronicity

Gap size
Moderate — outcomes dramatically improved but cure remains elusive; CAR-T access critical unmet need
Primary barrier
access + biological
Barrier detail: BCMA-targeted CAR-T therapy is transformative but manufacturing delays (6+ weeks), $420K+ cost, and limited centre access restrict use; bispecific antibodies (teclistamab) offer alternative; a functional cure remains an aspiration not a reality for most patients

Last SoC change: 2024 — talquetamab (GPRC5D bispecific) approved; 2022 ciltacabtagene autoleucel CAR-T approved; 2021 idecabtagene vicleucel CAR-T first-ever for MM; 2019 daratumumab as frontline; bortezomib/lenalidomide backbone since 2003-2006
