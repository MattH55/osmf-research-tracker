Remission & chronicity

Spontaneous remission
Spontaneous PASI90 remission essentially zero without treatment; natural fluctuation occurs but complete clearance rare; <5% achieve sustained spontaneous remission over 5 years
Best-intervention remission
IL-17/IL-23 biologics achieve PASI100 (complete skin clearance) in 40-87% depending on agent and timeframe; complete clearance is now a realistic treatment goal, not a rarity

Gap size
Large — most patients never reach biologic therapy despite clear eligibility; on-drug clearance rates are exceptional but drug-free remission after cessation is short (12-32 weeks median for PASI loss)
Primary barrier
access/practice
Barrier detail: Most moderate-severe psoriasis patients remain on topicals or older systemics (methotrexate, cyclosporine) for years before biologics are offered; prior authorisation barriers significant; psoriasis is associated with cardiovascular risk requiring systemic treatment beyond skin clearance

Last SoC change: 2020-2022 — IL-23 inhibitors (risankizumab 2019, guselkumab 2017) established complete clearance as the new standard goal; IL-17 inhibitors since 2015; prior biologics (anti-TNF, ustekinumab) since 2009; methotrexate first-line unchanged since 1960s
