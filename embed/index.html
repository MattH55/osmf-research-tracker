Script tag attributes

                

                    
AttributeValues

                    
                        
data-conditionOne key or a comma list: long-covid, me-cfs, pacvs, lyme, gulf-war-illness, pots, mcas, other-post-viral

                        
data-limit1 to 25 (default 5). With several conditions the newest across all are merged.

                        
data-themelight (default) or dark

                        
data-titleOptional heading override

                    
                

                
Good to know

                
    
                    
  • No global CSS: styling is isolated in a shadow root, so it will not change your page and your page will not change it.
    • 
                    
  • Fails silently: if the feed cannot be reached, visitors see a single plain link rather than an error.
    • 
                    
  • Attribution link ("via Open Source Medicine Research Tracker") is part of the licence for using the widget; please keep it.
    • 
                    
  • Programmatic use: OSMResearchWidget.render(element, {condition, limit, theme}) after loading the script with data-manual.
