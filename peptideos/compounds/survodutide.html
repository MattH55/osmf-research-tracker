Survodutide

Synthetic lipidated peptide; full structure not verified for this record.

Non-standard residues or modifications: no plain one-letter sequence

Codes: BI 456906 · CAS 2805997-46-8

An investigational synthetic peptide from Boehringer Ingelheim that activates the glucagon and GLP-1 receptors. It is in phase 3 development for obesity and for metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease, including steatohepatitis with fibrosis or cirrhosis. It is not approved in any jurisdiction checked.

02What the human evidence shows

E1

Weight reduction in obesity or overweight

Two published phase 3 trials and one phase 2 trial. SYNCHRONIZE-1 (725 adults without diabetes, 76 weeks): weight change -12.2% and -13.0% in the two dose groups versus -5.4% with placebo. SYNCHRONIZE-2 (752 adults with type 2 diabetes, 76 weeks): -8.2% and -9.8% versus -3.9%. The phase 2 trial (387 adults, 46 weeks) reported -6.2% to -14.9% versus -2.8%, with only 60% completing treatment.

One phase 3 trial (SYNCHRONIZE-MASLD, 216 adults, 48 weeks). A reduction of at least 30% in MRI-measured liver fat occurred in 68.5% with survodutide versus 28.6% with placebo (treatment-regimen estimand). Liver fat is an imaging surrogate; the authors note short duration and recruitment limited to the US and Spain.

03Safety signals

S2Substantial controlled-trial safety data

There are no adequate human safety data. The absence of reported harm is not evidence of safety.

  • Gastrointestinal adverse events [1]
    In SYNCHRONIZE-1, gastrointestinal adverse events occurred in 80.9% and 89.7% of the two survodutide groups versus 47.9% with placebo.
  • High treatment discontinuation in dose-finding [3]
    In the phase 2 obesity trial, 61% of survodutide recipients completed the 46-week treatment period (60% on placebo); gastrointestinal adverse events occurred in 75% versus 42%.

04What is unknown

  • Cardiovascular safety result: SYNCHRONIZE-CVOT (NCT06077864) is completed on ClinicalTrials.gov but no publication was located as of 2026-10-10
  • Whether liver-fat and histology improvements translate into fewer cirrhosis or liver-related events; LIVERAGE trials are recruiting
  • Safety beyond 76 weeks of exposure, the longest duration in published phase 3 trials
  • Effects of glucagon receptor agonism on heart rate, lean mass and amino-acid metabolism over the long term in humans
  • Identity and purity of any material sold outside trials; no lawful supply exists and no assay data were located

07Mechanism

Survodutide is a peptide agonist of both the glucagon receptor and the GLP-1 receptor.

Mechanism is placed last on purpose: a plausible mechanism is not evidence that a compound works in people.
