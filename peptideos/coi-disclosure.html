Governance

Conflict of interest disclosure

PeptideOS is published by the Open Source Medicine Foundation (OSMF). This page states who is responsible for it, how it is paid for and what it refuses.

What PeptideOS refuses

  • Funding of any kind from peptide vendors, manufacturers, compounding pharmacies or supplement companies, including “educational grants”.
  • Advertising, sponsored content, affiliate links and referral fees.
  • Any arrangement that lets a funder see or change a record before publication.

If any of this ever changes, this page changes first.

Funding

PeptideOS has no dedicated external funding. It is maintained as part of OSMF’s open research work. Specific funding sources, if any are received, will be listed here with amounts and terms before the money is used.

An earlier version of this page listed grants and amounts that could not be substantiated. It was withdrawn on 2026-10-10; see the corrections log.

Responsibility

Editorial responsibility rests with OSMF. Each compound record names its reviewer and review date. Records are researched with AI assistance under a written protocol and machine-checked against Crossref, ClinicalTrials.gov and primary regulator sources before publication.

Related work

OSMF also runs clinical research of its own. If OSMF ever studies a compound listed here, the compound page will say so at the top.
