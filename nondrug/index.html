How this map is built

  1. Source. For each condition, ClinicalTrials.gov is searched for interventional studies with a behavioural, device, dietary-supplement, "other" or procedure intervention, plus a second search for named supplements that registries often record as drugs (vitamin D, omega-3, probiotics, melatonin and similar). Results are sorted by last update; up to 1,000 trials per search are kept.
  2. Which interventions count. The registry's own intervention type decides first: medicines and biologicals are excluded, except clearly named supplements. Behavioural and supplement interventions are included; devices, procedures and "other" interventions are included only when their name matches one of the classes on this page (so stents, catheters and surgery are left out).
  3. On-topic only. The registry's condition search expands terms loosely (a Long COVID search also returns post-stroke fatigue trials), so a trial counts for a condition only when its registered conditions or title name that condition or a listed synonym. 7,543 off-topic matches were removed.
  4. Controls are not counted. An intervention that appears only in placebo, sham, active-comparator or no-intervention arms is a control, not the thing being tested, and is excluded.
  5. Excluded in this build: 40,262 medicine or biological interventions, 7,144 control-only interventions and 55,452 other entries (assessments, usual care, procedures and devices that match no class).
  6. No efficacy grades. Status labels describe the trial record only. "Results posted" means the sponsor uploaded results to ClinicalTrials.gov; it does not mean the intervention worked.
  7. Capped searches. For these conditions the registry returned more trials than the cap, so only the most recently updated are included: Anxiety (1,000 of 8,377), Asthma (1,000 of 1,668), Atrial Fibrillation (1,000 of 1,978), Attention Deficit-Hyperactivity Disorder (1,000 of 1,012), Breast Cancer (1,000 of 6,004), Cancer (1,000 of 36,329), Cervical Cancer (1,000 of 3,707), Chronic Kidney Disease (1,000 of 2,122), Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (1,000 of 2,688), Colorectal Cancer (1,000 of 3,581), Diabetic Neuropathy (1,000 of 1,016), HIV/AIDS (1,000 of 3,932), Heart Failure (1,000 of 3,403), Inflammatory Bowel Disease (1,000 of 1,303), Insomnia (1,000 of 2,441), Ischemic Stroke (1,000 of 1,665), Low Back Pain (1,000 of 2,509), Lung Cancer (1,000 of 3,244), Major Depressive Disorder (1,000 of 1,850), Multiple Sclerosis (1,000 of 1,529), Non-Hodgkin Lymphoma (1,000 of 1,459), Obesity (1,000 of 9,481), Obstructive Sleep Apnea (1,000 of 1,476), Osteoarthritis (1,000 of 3,706), Ovarian Cancer (1,000 of 1,479), Pancreatic Cancer (1,000 of 1,331), Parkinson Disease (1,000 of 2,379), Peripheral Arterial Disease (1,000 of 1,163), Prostate Cancer (1,000 of 2,475), Schizophrenia (1,000 of 1,859), Sepsis (1,000 of 1,038), Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus (1,000 of 2,071), Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (1,000 of 4,934).
  8. Known limits. Classes come from intervention names and can misfile an individual entry; the registry record is authoritative. A trial can appear under several classes and several conditions.
