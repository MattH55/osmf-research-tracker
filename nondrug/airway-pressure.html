Positive airway pressure and oral appliances

255 registered trials across 29 conditions, from ClinicalTrials.gov.

  1. Research Tracker
  2. Non-drug evidence map
  3. Positive airway pressure and oral appliances

Machines that keep the airway open during sleep, and mouth splints for sleep apnoea. This page lists the registered trials that test it, condition by condition, with each trial's status on ClinicalTrials.gov.

⚠️
Research information, not medical advice. These pages index what has been studied in registered trials. A trial existing, completing or posting results says nothing on its own about whether an approach works or is safe for you. Supplements and devices can interact with medicines. Discuss any change with your own clinician.
