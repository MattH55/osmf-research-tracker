326 registered trials across 66 conditions, from ClinicalTrials.gov.

Live bacteria or yeasts, and the fibres that feed them, taken to change the gut microbiome. This page lists the registered trials that test it, condition by condition, with each trial's status on ClinicalTrials.gov.

⚠️
Research information, not medical advice. These pages index what has been studied in registered trials. A trial existing, completing or posting results says nothing on its own about whether an approach works or is safe for you. Supplements and devices can interact with medicines. Discuss any change with your own clinician.
