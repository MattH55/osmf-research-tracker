Research Tracker / Chronic Disease Interventions / Ankylosing Spondylitis / Axial Spondyloarthropathy

Remission & chronicity

Spontaneous remission
Progressive structural damage (sacroiliac fusion, syndesmophytes) in majority without treatment; spontaneous radiographic remission essentially zero; ~10% achieve clinical remission without anti-inflammatory therapy
Best-intervention remission
TNF inhibitors (infliximab, adalimumab): ASAS20 response in 60-70%, clinical remission (ASDAS-ID) in 20-40%; IL-17 inhibitors (secukinumab, ixekizumab): equivalent or superior efficacy; remission rates with early biologics in non-radiographic axSpA may reach 40-50%
Gap size
Large — most patients spend years on NSAIDs before biologic assessment; diagnostic delay averages 7-10 years
Primary barrier
practice/access
Diagnostic delay of 7-10 years from symptom onset is the most significant modifiable factor; MRI-based diagnosis of nr-axSpA (non-radiographic) can enable earlier biologic initiation before structural damage; HLA-B27 testing remains underused in primary care assessment of inflammatory back pain
