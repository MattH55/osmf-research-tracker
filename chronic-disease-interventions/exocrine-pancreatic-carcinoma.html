Research Tracker / Chronic Disease Interventions / Pancreatic Cancer

Remission & chronicity

Spontaneous remission
Essentially zero; one of the highest cancer mortality rates; 5-year survival ~12% overall; only 15-20% have resectable disease at diagnosis
Best-intervention remission
Resectable: surgery (Whipple) + FOLFIRINOX adjuvant: 54.4 months median OS (PRODIGE 24, vs 35.0 months gemcitabine); BRCA-mutant metastatic: olaparib maintenance after platinum-based: 7.4 vs 3.8 months PFS (POLO); neoadjuvant chemotherapy: may convert 20-30% of borderline-resectable to resectable
Gap size
Very large — minimal survival improvement for most patients over past 20 years; imaging and liquid biopsy for early detection is the biggest opportunity
Primary barrier
biological + access (early detection)
Pancreatic cancer is detected late (70-80% at Stage III-IV) due to absence of early symptoms and screening; CA19-9 is insufficiently sensitive/specific for early detection; BRCA1/2 and other HRR mutation testing is not universal; KRAS G12D/G12V targeted agents represent a genuine pipeline advance
