Remission & chronicity

Spontaneous remission
Spontaneous clearance in <30% of acute cases; chronic infection rarely clears without treatment (<1%/year)
Best-intervention remission
>95% cured (sustained virologic response at 12 weeks) with 8-12 week courses of direct-acting antivirals (DAAs)
Gap size
Near-zero biological gap; large access/utilization gap
Primary barrier
access
Only 65% of eligible patients with private insurance received DAA treatment as of 2020; rates declining since 2019; Medicaid restrictions remain in many states; $20,000+ drug cost for generics unavailable in US
