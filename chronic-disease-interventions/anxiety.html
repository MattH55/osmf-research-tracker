Research Tracker / Chronic Disease Interventions / Anxiety Disorders

Remission & chronicity

Spontaneous remission
GAD: spontaneous remission in ~25-30% at 1 year without treatment; social anxiety: 36% remission at 12 years untreated; panic disorder: 50-65% spontaneous partial remission but relapse common; 40-60% remain symptomatic at 5 years
Best-intervention remission
CBT (gold standard): 60-80% treatment response, 40-60% full remission at 12-24 weeks; combination CBT + SSRI superior to either alone in some disorders; exposure therapy for specific phobias: 90%+ response; SSRI/SNRI alone: 50-60% response
Gap size
Large — CBT is consistently superior to medication for long-term outcomes but is massively underused relative to pharmacotherapy
Primary barrier
practice/access
SSRIs/SNRIs are first-line by default in most primary care settings; CBT is guideline-recommended but wait times of 6-18 months are common; digital CBT (iCBT) is evidence-based but rarely prescribed; benzodiazepines continue to be prescribed long-term despite dependence risk
