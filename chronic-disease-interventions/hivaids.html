Remission & chronicity

Spontaneous remission
Spontaneous elite control (natural viremic suppression without ART) in <1% of people living with HIV; untreated, median time to AIDS ~10 years; viral rebound within weeks of ART interruption in 99%+ of patients
Best-intervention remission
Modern ART (integrase inhibitor-based): viral suppression to undetectable in >95% within 6 months, maintained indefinitely on treatment; life expectancy near-normalised with early treatment; 7 documented sterilising cures worldwide (all via CCR5-Δ32 stem cell transplantation); post-treatment controllers (VISCONTI cohort): ~5-15% of early-treated individuals achieve durable ART-free viremic suppression
Gap size
Very large (sterilising cure); near-zero biological gap for quality-of-life control on ART; large access gap globally
Primary barrier
biological (sterilising cure) + access (ART, particularly in LMIC)
ART is the most successful chronic disease management story in medicine but is definitionally not a cure; 39 million people living with HIV globally, only 77% on ART, only 72% virally suppressed (2023 UNAIDS); broadly neutralising antibodies (bNAbs) showing promising functional remission signals (ACTG A5386, 2025)
