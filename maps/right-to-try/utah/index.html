Provide Manufacturer Liability Protection Under RTTdo not provide liability shieldUtah Code § 58-85-104“A physician, other licensed health care provider, or hospital that treats an eligible patient ... under this chapter may not, for any harm done to the eligible patient by the investigational drug or device, be subject to: (a) civil liability”Note: The liability protection covers only physicians, other licensed providers and hospitals. As to manufacturers, § 58-85-104(3)(c)(ii) only says the chapter does not create a private right of action 'against a manufacturer, for the manufacturer's refusal to provide' the drug; it has no clause shielding manufacturers from liability for harm. The § 58-85-106 public health emergency immunity likewise covers only health care providers. So false for manufacturers.Link2026-10-09high
